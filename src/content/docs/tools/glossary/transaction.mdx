A transaction records a payment or a related financial operation, such as a
refund or chargeback. It includes the amount, currency, and status so you can
track the result and match it to your sale or order.

A transaction can include:

- The amount and currency
- Its processing status
- The payment type and entry mode
- SumUp and merchant-provided identifiers
- Card, device, payout, refund, and other event details where applicable

The [sale](/tools/glossary/sale/) records what was sold, while the
[checkout](/tools/glossary/checkout/) coordinates collecting payment. The
transaction records the processing result, which you can use to reconcile the
payment with your sale or order.

## Transaction lifecycle

The `status` field describes the current state of a payment transaction:

| Status       | Meaning                                                         |
| ------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `PENDING`    | The transaction exists, but its final outcome is not yet known. |
| `SUCCESSFUL` | The transaction completed successfully.                         |
| `FAILED`     | The payment attempt did not complete successfully.              |
| `CANCELLED`  | The transaction was canceled or reversed before completion.     |
| `REFUNDED`   | The transaction was refunded in full or in part.                |

Transaction status is separate from checkout status. For example, a successful
transaction has status `SUCCESSFUL`, while the paid online checkout has status
`PAID`.

`REFUNDED` can mean a partial or full refund. Check `refunded_amount` in
transaction history to see the total amount refunded.

Related events, such as refunds, chargebacks, payouts, and payout deductions,
have their own types and statuses. A successful payment does not by itself
mean its funds have been paid out to the merchant.

## Transactions in the public APIs

Use the Transactions API to:

- [Retrieve a transaction](/api/transactions/get/) by its SumUp ID, transaction
  code, foreign transaction ID, or client transaction ID
- [List transactions](/api/transactions/list/) for a merchant and filter the
  history by status, payment type, entry mode, or transaction type
- [Refund a transaction](/api/transactions/refund/) in full or partially

Transaction history distinguishes payments, refunds, and chargebacks through
the `type` field. The transaction details also include related financial events
in `events` and `transaction_events`.

## Relationship to a sale and checkout

A typical online flow starts with an order in your system. You create a
checkout for the amount to collect, then process it. The checkout links to
payment attempts through its `transactions` array. Successful synchronous
processing can also return `transaction_id` and `transaction_code`.

In-person payments initiated through a reader or mobile SDK also produce
transactions. Store SumUp identifiers alongside your own order references so
you can reconcile each transaction with the correct sale.
