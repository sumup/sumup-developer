A checkout is a request to collect a specific amount in a specific currency
for a merchant. You use it to accept payment for a
[sale](/tools/glossary/sale/) or an order in your system.

An online checkout commonly contains:

- The amount and currency to collect
- The merchant receiving the payment
- A merchant-defined reference
- Optional customer, expiration, redirect, and callback details
- Its current status and any linked
  [transactions](/tools/glossary/transaction/)

A checkout can exist before any payment attempt. Its creation alone does not
confirm payment: it can remain `PENDING`, fail, or expire without a successful
transaction.

## Checkout types in the public APIs

SumUp APIs support checkouts for online and in-person payments:

- The **Checkouts API** creates an online checkout, processes it with a payment
  instrument, and retrieves its latest state. See
  [Create a checkout](/api/checkouts/create/) and supported integrations such as
  the [Payment Widget](/online-payments/checkouts/card-widget/) or
  [Hosted Checkout](/online-payments/checkouts/hosted-checkout/).
- The **Readers API** creates a checkout on a paired card reader. This starts an
  asynchronous in-person payment flow. See
  [Create a reader checkout](/api/readers/create-checkout/).

Reader checkouts use different request and response fields from online
checkouts. Acceptance of a reader checkout request does not confirm payment;
follow the [Cloud API result flow](/terminal-payments/cloud-api/#receive-transaction-results)
to verify the outcome. The following sections describe online checkouts.

## Typical online checkout flow

Your backend creates a checkout and provides its ID to the payment UI. The
payment UI processes the payment, and SumUp links the resulting transaction
records to the checkout. Your backend retrieves the checkout to confirm the
latest payment state before updating your order.

For redirect-based payment methods or 3D Secure (3DS), processing can return a
next action instead of a final result. Use
[Retrieve a checkout](/api/checkouts/get/) from your backend to verify the
latest status, including after a
[checkout webhook notification](/online-payments/webhooks/). Only mark the
order as paid when the retrieved checkout has `status: "PAID"`.

## Relationship to a sale and transaction

- The **sale** records the items sold, their prices, and the associated payments.
- The **checkout** carries the amount, currency, and instructions needed to
  attempt payment.
- The **transaction** records the payment attempt's outcome and related
  financial events.

A newly created checkout can have an empty `transactions` array. Payment
processing attaches transaction records as attempts occur. Use the checkout to
manage the payment flow; use the
[Transactions API](/api/transactions/get/) for transaction details, history,
and post-payment operations.
