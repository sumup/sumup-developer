A sale records what a merchant sold to a customer and how the customer paid.
It brings together the items, prices, and payments for a purchase.

A sale can include:

- Items and quantities
- Prices, taxes, and discounts
- The sales channel
- The total amount and associated payments

## How sales, checkouts, and transactions relate

Use these terms to describe different parts of the same flow:

| Term                                        | Purpose                                                                 |
| ------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Sale                                        | Records what the customer is buying and the associated payments.        |
| [Checkout](/tools/glossary/checkout/)       | Carries the amount, currency, and instructions used to collect payment. |
| [Transaction](/tools/glossary/transaction/) | Records the outcome of a payment attempt and related financial events.  |

In a typical online purchase, your system holds the order or basket. You create
a checkout for the amount to collect, and payment processing produces a
transaction. You then verify the payment result before updating your order.

The exact relationship depends on the integration. A merchant can split one
sale across multiple payments, and a cash payment does not require an online
checkout.

## Sales in the public API

The [Sales API](/api/tags/sales/) exposes fully paid, closed sales, including
item lines and a `payments` array. It does not return unfinished or unpaid
sales. Keep track of unpaid orders or baskets in your own system.

A sale's payment entries support bookkeeping and reconciliation. Use the
[Transactions API](/api/transactions/get/) for the processing details and
financial events of a SumUp transaction.
