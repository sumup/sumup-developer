Save a customer's card as a tokenized payment instrument, then use that token for later payments covered by the customer's consent. The Payment Widget collects card details and handles authentication; your server stores the customer and payment references.

## Build the integration

1. [Create a customer](/online-payments/guides/saved-cards/customers/) with an ID from your own system.
2. [Save a card](/online-payments/guides/saved-cards/save-card/) with a setup checkout and the Payment Widget.
3. [Charge the saved card](/online-payments/guides/saved-cards/recurring-payments/) through a new checkout for each payment.
