Embed in-person payments in your mobile app with a Bluetooth card reader or Tap to Pay. Choose your platform, then follow its quickstart or the guide for your next task.

## Available SDKs

| SDK                                                                   | Readers supported              | Tap to Pay                      |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ | ------------------------------- |
| [iOS SDK](/terminal-payments/sdks/ios/)                               | Solo Lite, Solo, Air, 3G, PIN+ | Included in the same SDK        |
| [Android reader SDK](/terminal-payments/sdks/android/)                | Solo Lite, Solo, Air, 3G, PIN+ | Use the separate Tap to Pay SDK |
| [Android Tap to Pay SDK](/terminal-payments/sdks/android/tap-to-pay/) | No external reader             | Yes                             |

## How It Works

The SDK provides the payment UI and handles communication with the reader or the phone's payment interface. Your app starts checkout and handles the returned payment result.
