Generate more bookings with less effort. Our integration options ensure a streamlined card payment flow and costless receipt solution. Personalize your payment experience by accommodating checkouts within your own application.

## Our merchants' most valued features

- **[Native SDK](/terminal-payments/tools/sdk/)** for more control in the merchant app, available for Android and iOS.

- **[Receipt REST API](/api/receipts/)** for customized receipts and the option of skipping the success screen.

- **[Refund REST API](/api/transactions/refund-a-transaction)** allows refunds within your app, having been performed up to 180 days after the payment took place, and with the refund amount available in the SumUp Profile payout balance.

<Aside type="tip">

Omnichannel? You can include e-commerce in your payment range by using our [Online Payments API](/online-payments/introduction/get-started).

</Aside>
