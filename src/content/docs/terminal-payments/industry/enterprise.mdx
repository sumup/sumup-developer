Our integration portfolio provides enterprise level payment solutions for business needs at a larger scale. We support you balance your daily operations with detailed transaction history, multiple account management and many more services.

## Our merchants' most valued features

- **[Native SDK](/terminal-payments/tools/sdk/)** for more control in the merchant app, available for Android and iOS.

- **[Authorization code flow](/online-payments/authorization#authorization-code-flow)** for faster connection to the app, by generating an access token using OAuth 2.0. This will authenticate user accounts without requiring SumUp credentials each time they launch the app.

- **[Subaccount REST API](/api/subaccounts/)** for creating and managing multiple employee accounts within your app's dashboard. Backend communication reflects the accounts in the [SumUp dashboard](https://me.sumup.com/), where access to reports and their reconciliation can be made on a subaccount level.
