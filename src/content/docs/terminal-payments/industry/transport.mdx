Card payments in the transport industry have become fundamental. Our card terminals guarantee you mobility and configurations that ensure seamless performance accommodating all of your needs.

## Our merchants' most valued features

- **[Native SDK](/terminal-payments/tools/sdk/)** for more control in the merchant app, available for Android and iOS.

- **[Authorization code flow](/online-payments/authorization/#authorization-code-flow)** for faster connection to the app, by generating an access token using OAuth 2.0. This will authenticate user accounts without requiring SumUp credentials each time they launch the app.

- **[Subaccount REST API](/api/subaccounts/)** for creating and managing multiple agent accounts within your app's dashboard. Backend communication reflects the accounts in the [SumUp dashboard](https://me.sumup.com/), where access to reports and their reconciliation can be made on a subaccount level.

- **[Transaction REST API](/api/transactions/)** as a second source of verification for transaction statuses.
