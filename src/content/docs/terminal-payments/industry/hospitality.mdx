As a restaurant, café or bar, providing a fast and reliable card payment method is essential. You will have the possibility to offer your customers and their staff a fast and seamless card payment solution, guaranteed to satisfy the cardholders.

## Our merchants' most valued features

- **[Native SDK](/terminal-payments/tools/sdk/)** for more control in the merchant app, available for Android and iOS.

- **[Subaccount REST API](/api/subaccounts/)** for creating and managing multiple attendant accounts within your app's dashboard. Backend communication reflects the accounts in the [SumUp dashboard](https://me.sumup.com/), where access to reports and their reconciliation can be made on a subaccount level.

- **[Receipt REST API](/api/receipts/)** for customized receipts and the option of skipping the success screen.

<Aside type="tip">

Omnichannel? You can include e-commerce to your payment range by using our [Online Payments API](/online-payments/)

</Aside>
