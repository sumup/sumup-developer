---
title: Can I get a test profile?
---

To get started with SumUp first [login](/login) (if you don't have a SumUp profile you will first need to register). To activate your SumUp Profile follow the [Activate my profile](https://help.sumup.com/en-GB/articles/7gSOGSm2wEwIHyAvjitrLF) steps.

Once you have an active SumUp Profile, go to https://developer.sumup.com/, navigate to the Test profile setup page by clicking on "Create test account" on the right side of the navigation, and then create your profile by click on the "Setup test profile" button. Your Test profile will be created for the same country as your merchant.

Once your profile is generated copy and store safely the user name (format: 32 characters@developers.sumup.com) and randomly generated unique password.

**Note:** You will not be able to access your password afterwards make sure to store it safely before dismissing the message
